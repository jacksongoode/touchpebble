# Touchpebble — Store Listing

## Short description

Your Touchstone Climbing check-in QR on your Pebble — no pulling out your phone
at the front desk.

## Long description

Touchpebble puts your Touchstone check-in QR on your wrist: raise your watch,
scan, done.

- **Always-valid QR** — auto-refreshes every 8 seconds so it never goes stale.
- **Backlight on** — scans cleanly in any lighting.
- **One-time setup** — paste your portal cookies once from the Pebble app's
  Settings and you're signed in indefinitely.
- **Private by design** — cookies stay on your phone, nothing is stored
  server-side; the proxy is open source and self-hostable.

## Notes

- Requires a Touchstone Climbing membership login
